## Scope and mode

Persuade. The shared industry-page system for New Jersey manufacturers and defense suppliers, professional-services firms, law firms, accounting and tax firms, independent medical and dental practices, and contractors and trades.

## Audience, job, and action

Owners and operating leaders need to recognize the business risk, understand how Helm Mail, Helm Watch, and Helm Ready divide the work, and choose one low-friction next step. Manufacturing leads to a CMMC readiness call. Professional services, law, accounting, and contractors lead to the free public-domain scan. Medical practices lead to a HIPAA readiness call.

## Proof and constraints

Use primary or authoritative sources with scope qualifiers beside every number. Keep all vendor brands off customer-facing pages. Helm Watch covers eligible Windows and Mac devices under standard scope; phones, tablets, servers, shop-floor systems, and other device classes are separately scoped. Helm Mail does not provide secure-message delivery or secure file transfer. Never imply that a Helm product alone satisfies CMMC, HIPAA, insurance, or customer requirements, and never imply that Helm certifies compliance.

## Direction and memorable moment

Inherit the established Quiet Helm House system. Each page moves from a plain-language risk ledger into a ruled evidence section, then connects the relevant Helm services to the buyer's actual workflow. Industry heroes use distinct code-native SVG scenes at a large scale comparable to manufacturing's Aero scene: law uses large balancing scales that settle into an even resting state, with stronger light-theme strokes; accounting uses a full reconciliation workspace with a sidebar, summary cards, paired entries, a scan, trend chart, total, and verification state; medical and dental use a raised portable bedside monitor with a handle, controls, pulse, right-side medical-cross and tooth-care status nodes, records, a privacy shield, and a thick telescoping support. Desktop keeps each scene bounded and right-aligned, with the clinical support continuing into the bottom of the hero. Phones place the scene in its own in-flow panel between the headline and supporting copy so no text covers the animation. All hero scenes pause off-screen and remain complete and static under reduced motion. The memorable moments are the industry-specific hero motion and the evidence row that pairs a large, qualified fact with its direct source.

## Unresolved decisions

Hospitals remain out of scope until Helm validates an enterprise healthcare service model. Page performance should be reviewed against qualified scans, readiness calls, and opportunities before expanding the top-level industry taxonomy again.
