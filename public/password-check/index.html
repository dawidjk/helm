Has this password appeared in known exposure data?

This checks a password value against Have I Been Pwned’s Pwned Passwords service. It does not look up an email address, domain or account.

What is sent when you check

On your submission, this page computes a lookup hash locally and sends its first five hexadecimal characters to HIBP over HTTPS. HIBP receives that short prefix and your connection information, including your IP address. The password and full hash stay in your browser.

This page has no analytics, advertising pixels, chat or form-capture scripts. It does not store the password or hash. The input clears after submission and when the page is hidden or left.

The field becomes available when the local checker starts. If it stays disabled, reload the page or use a current browser over HTTPS.

The check uses exactly what you enter, including spaces and capitalization. Nothing is sent while you type.

What a result means

A match means this password appears in known exposed-password data. It does not establish that your account was compromised. No match is not proof that the password is safe.

If there is a match, change the password through the service’s trusted settings and replace it wherever you reused it. A password manager can help you choose unique passwords. See our MFA guide for additional account protection.

Read HIBP’s Pwned Passwords documentation for how the lookup works.
